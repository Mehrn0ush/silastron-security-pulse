• Confirmed exploitation means CISA listed the CVE in the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog. KEV indicates confirmed historical exploitation, not current exposure.
  • EPSS is an estimated probability of exploitation, not confirmation. EPSS is currently not published here. FIRST grants public use of its scores with requested attribution but has not published an explicit licence to redistribute them in a derived public dataset, and this site would republish them as JSON consumed by third parties. Until that is clarified the EPSS column shows "Disabled", which is not a probability of zero. This is a licensing decision, not a data fault.
  • Severity is source-attributed CVSS. CNA, NVD, and CISA ADP assessments are shown separately when they exist. Display order prefers a newer CVSS version, then CNA, NVD, CISA ADP; that is a UI preference, not a correctness ranking. Missing scores stay unavailable.
  • CISA SSVC decision points (Exploitation, Automatable, Technical impact) are copied from the CISA ADP (Vulnrichment) container of the CVE record, with CISA's assessment date. They are shown as separate facts, never combined into a score or priority. Missing SSVC means CISA has not added it, not low impact.
  • Collection outcome (ok, degraded, or failed) is whether the collector finished. A degraded snapshot succeeded; it is not a failed collection.
  • Record completeness is per CVE: complete extracted inventory, partial truncated product/version text, or unavailable enrichment. Completeness refers to extracted data, not proof of complete real-world affectedness. These are not the collection outcome and are not freshness.
  • Product affectedness is vendor/product text from KEV and CVE records. A complete extracted inventory does not prove complete real-world product coverage or deployment exposure.
  • Data status separates last successful source verification, last successful collection, last published snapshot, and last upstream update. An unchanged but re-validated KEV catalog is not labeled stale. Snapshot age is not verification.
  • What's Changed compares the current published snapshot to the immediately preceding published snapshot. It is not a collect-time candidate diff and is not CISA's KEV dateAdded list.
  • No composite risk score is calculated. Signals stay independent.
  • Deployment exposure is not assessed here.
