How to read these signals

  • Confirmed exploitation means CISA listed the CVE in the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog.
  • EPSS, when present, is an estimated probability of exploitation, not confirmation.
  • Severity is CVSS from the CVE CNA container when available.
  • Collection outcome (ok, degraded, or failed) is whether the collector finished. A degraded snapshot succeeded; it is not a failed collection.
  • Record completeness is per CVE: complete extracted inventory, partial truncated product/version text, or unavailable enrichment. These are not the collection outcome and are not freshness.
  • Product affectedness is vendor/product text from KEV and CVE records. A complete extracted inventory does not prove complete real-world product coverage or deployment exposure.
  • Freshness is computed at viewing time from the snapshot timestamp. It is independent of collection outcome and record completeness.
  • Deployment exposure is not assessed here.
